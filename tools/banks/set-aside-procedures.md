# Punishment and restrictive procedures set aside from the TD-1 and GB-1 banks

These studies used a punishment or restrictive procedure. The banks left it out: a protocol's steps or an objective never include it, and some studies were skipped entirely. This list keeps them on record for a future punishment and punishment-assessment form, for the rare case where, with proper review and approval, one of these procedures may be a justified part of an intervention.

- **Tier A** procedures could be considered only in rare cases, after a functional assessment, after less restrictive procedures have been tried, and with the review and consent the district requires.
- **Tier B** procedures are never used in schools. They are listed only so the form can show them as prohibited.
