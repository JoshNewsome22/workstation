Student and Health Record

What this record is, and is not

It keeps what happened, what was done, what the doctor said and who was told, in one place with the case. It does not replace the school’s or agency’s own incident or accident report, the nurse’s record, or a report of suspected abuse or neglect. In an emergency, follow the student’s health plan and call 911; write here afterwards.

    Photos print only when that box is ticked, and it is unticked each time the form opens. Attached papers (PDF) are listed by name on the printout; they are not printed with it.

    What This Form Is For

    One record for each medical event that needed more than routine care at school or on school time: something swallowed, a seizure that was long or intense, a serious injury, an allergic reaction, choking or trouble breathing, or an illness that ended with a doctor or an emergency room. The team can see what happened, what the doctor said, who was told and what still has to be done, without asking the family twice.

    It is not the official report

    The school’s or agency’s own incident or accident report, the nurse’s record and any report of suspected abuse or neglect are made as their own rules say, by the people those rules name. This record sits beside them. In an emergency, follow the student’s health plan and call 911; write here afterwards.

    Writing an Event

    • Kind. Choosing the kind adds the questions that kind needs: for a seizure, when it started and ended and whether rescue medicine was given; for something swallowed, what it was, how many and where it came from; for an injury, the body part and how it happened.
    • What happened. Write what was seen and done, in plain words, in the order it happened. Write who saw it. Do not write a diagnosis: write what the doctor said under What the doctor said, in the doctor’s words or the discharge papers’ words.
    • Times. A seizure’s start and end give its length. The record shows the length; the student’s seizure action plan says what to do and when to call 911.
    • Who was told. The parent or guardian, an administrator, the BCBA, and whether the school’s own report was made.
    • Back at school. The date, whether a doctor’s clearance was needed and received, and any limits on activity.
    • After the event. What may have contributed, and what was changed. The form names the other forms worth opening: for something swallowed, Form DM-1’s safety precautions, the item pool on Form PA-1 and the pica safety checklist in Form TD-1’s protocol bank.
    • Follow-up. Each thing still to do, with its date. What is still open shows on the Events page and reaches the workstation’s lists.

    Photos and Papers

    • Papers. Discharge papers and a doctor’s note can be attached as a PDF, or as photos of the pages. A PDF may be up to 8 MB.
    • Photos. A photo is made smaller before it is kept. A photo of the student (an injury) can be added only when Consent to photograph the student is answered Yes. A photo of an item or of papers does not need that consent. Never photograph a private area.
    • Where they are kept. On this device, and inside the case file when the case is saved. They do not go into the case facts other forms read, the CSV, the staff pages or the reply box. They go where the case file goes: decide where that is as you do for the rest of the student’s record.
    • Printing. Photos print only when Include the photos when printing is ticked on the Events page. Attached PDFs are listed by name.
    • This form’s own file. Save data writes this form’s file with the photos and papers inside it. In the workstation, Save case carries them with the case.

    Late Entries

    Each event keeps the time it was first written. One written more than 24 hours after it happened is marked Late entry on screen and on paper. Write it anyway: a late record is better than none.

    About the simulation

    Load simulation fills the form with three invented events for training: something swallowed, a long seizure and a fall. Every name and detail is made up, and its one picture is drawn by the form.
