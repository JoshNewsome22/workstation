Licence issuer

This page makes the licence keys that the workstation checks. It works on this device only: the signing key is made here and nothing is sent anywhere. A customer never gets this page.

1. Your signing key

No signing key is open.

The signing key is one file, locked with a passphrase you choose. Whoever has the file and the passphrase can make licences, so keep the file in two safe places and tell no one the passphrase. If both copies are lost, licences already made keep working, but new ones need a new key and a new version of the workstation.

2. A licence

Send the customer the key or the file. One person pastes the key in the workstation (Help, Licence). For every device at once, the person who looks after their website puts the file licence.key in the workstation's folder, beside index.html.
