The Student and the Training

The Presenter

Draft the Script from the Case

The draft takes what the case already holds: the student (the packet), the target behaviors and their definitions (Form TB-1), the function and its hypothesis statements (Form FS-1), the goals (Form GB-1) and the reinforcer menu (Form PA-1). Each part becomes a segment, with what you will say and the card that shows while you say it. The parts the case cannot know (strengths, the proactive strategies, the response plan, the takeaways) are drafted as prompts in [square brackets] for you to write. Draft from the case adds the segments you tick to the end of the script; nothing already written is changed.

The Look of the Graphics

The Script

Each row is one card, as a row of the Google Sheet: the segment (column A, the panel's title), the paragraphs (B), the heading (C, in gold) and the list (D), with two pictures and their captions (E, F). The teleprompter shows the paragraphs, or the row's own teleprompter words when you say more than the card. Tick Same words as above when the card changes while you are still in the same paragraph: the teleprompter keeps the paragraph and moves to the next card. Words in [square brackets] are still to be written. A card's text is one line a point; a line starting with • or - prints as a bullet.

0:00

A Bluetooth page-turner or keyboard works the teleprompter: Page Down, → or Enter for the next card (or the next point of a list that builds), Page Up or ← for the one before, Space to start or stop the scroll. Each card also changes the graphics window, when it is open. Scroll moves the script at the speed set (words a minute), while I speak (it moves only while the microphone hears you, and waits when you pause), or following my words (speech recognition on this device places each word you say in the script; skip a sentence or go back and it finds you). As the script scrolls the cards change with it, unless only the clicker changes them. Start the clock keeps the time each card came up (and, following your words, each word) for the chapters and the captions below.

In the words said: // marks a pause (‖), *a word* one to stress, and {a note} a note to yourself. They show on the teleprompter only: never on a card, in the sheet or in the captions. The microphone is used only while the script scrolls with your voice; nothing it hears is recorded or sent. Following your words needs the speech recogniser, about 57 MB, downloaded from this workstation's website the first time and kept on the device (not in the one-file edition).

The Graphics

Every card as it shows on the video, 1920 by 1080. Open the graphics window opens them in a window of their own, full screen on a second display (an iPad with Stage Manager can extend to a display, and a computer can send it out by HDMI), on the background chosen on Setup for the Yolobox to key out; the teleprompter changes it as you go. The layout of a card is chosen on its row in the Script (Automatic picks one from what the card holds).

What This Form Makes

The training video for a finished FBA and BIP, for a receiving team, new staff or a family: what you will say, and what is on screen while you say it. It replaces the spreadsheet of the script and the cards: the form drafts both from the case, keeps them together, runs the teleprompter, and draws the cards itself. Nothing leaves the device: the script, the cards and the pictures stay in this form's saved file.

1. DraftSetup: the student and the presenter. Draft from the case writes a segment for each part of the case the workstation holds (the target behaviors with their definitions, the function and its hypothesis, the goals, the reinforcers), with an opening and the parts to write yourself as prompts in [square brackets]. A sheet made before (the Google Sheet's first tab, downloaded as CSV, or the whole sheet as .xlsx) comes in through Import a sheet.
2. WriteScript: each row is a card, as a row of the sheet: the segment (A, the panel's title), the paragraphs (B, on the panel and on the teleprompter), the heading (C, in gold) and the list (D), with the teleprompter's own words when you say more than the card. The total time is worked out at the teleprompter's speed (about 140 words a minute). Words in [brackets] are counted as still to do.
3. Check the cardsGraphics: every card at the size of the video, in the look chosen on Setup. Chapters is the newer Flowics template: the panel with its title and headings in light gold, a chapter bar along its foot with the card's own chapter lit, and the series running as a ticker under it. Panel is the FBA and BIP template: the navy panel, the gold heading, the series band, the logo and the tag. Both are in Lato (and Merriweather for the bar and ticker), built in. Each row's layout can be changed (the panel, the full-width panel, a lower third, a section title, one or two pictures filling what the words leave, a picture beside the words, or no graphics). In the graphics window the panel stays from card to card and only its words fade, as in the template.
4. RecordTeleprompter on the iPad in front of the camera (mirrored for a beam-splitter glass), scrolling at the speed set, while you speak, or following your words; a clicker to go to the next card or the next point of a list that builds; the graphics window on a second display for the Yolobox. With the clock running, the take gives the video's YouTube chapters and its captions (SRT or WebVTT, the script's own words, timed by the cards or by the words heard). Or keep Flowics for now: Export for Sheets writes the workbook in the layout of the sheet it reads (the first tab every row in nine columns: the segment, the paragraphs, the heading, the list, the two picture captions and the three picture places; then a tab for each segment).

Privacy: the script names the student and describes the plan. In this form it stays on the device and in the saved file. A sheet exported for Flowics puts it in Google Sheets and Flowics; use the district's account and the services the district allows.
