Behaviors and targets

Every row here becomes a column on the data sheet and its own graph. A behavior measured by duration can be logged one episode at a time on the Daily data tab, and its daily total adds itself up. Guide: Recording Live at the Table

Timing the Session

Press Start when the observation begins and End when it is over. Pause stops the clock while the student is out of the room or the session is interrupted: the minutes written to the sheet leave the pauses out, and an episode or a cue still open at a pause is closed there. A planned length alerts you once, by sound, flash or vibration as set, and the clock runs on until End. The screen stays awake while the clock runs.

Recording Each Measure

  • Frequency and rate: tap +1 at each occurrence. A rate is the count over the minutes observed.
  • Duration and percent of time: tap when an episode starts and again when it ends. Each episode goes to the episode log with its start time and length, so one behavior’s episodes never overlap.
  • Latency: tap Cue given as you give the cue and Response when the student responds. The day’s value is the mean latency in minutes; a cue marked No response is left out of the mean and reported.
  • Interresponse time: tap Response at each response. The day’s value is the mean time between responses, in minutes; no interval spans a pause.
  • Percent correct, percent independent and prompt level: one tap per opportunity. Prompt levels are numbered in the order listed under the controls; edit the list to match the program.
  • Percent of intervals (momentary time sampling, partial or whole interval): record these with the observation timer on Form MT-1, which runs the marks and scores each interval by its rule, then type the day’s percent on the sheet.

A behavior’s own Undo takes back its last entry; Undo last entry, or Z, takes back the last entry of any behavior. A behavior left on Observing with no entries is written as 0, a measured zero; turn Observing off for a behavior you are not watching and it is left blank.

Saving to the Sheet

Nothing is written until Save to the sheet. The session goes into the row for the date shown (today unless you change it), added in date order if the sheet has none, under the condition the sheet has for that date. If the row already holds a value, choose for each behavior: Add (the default: a day’s total is the sum of its sessions, and a percent or a mean combines by its opportunities), Replace or Skip. A rate and a percent of time go with the observation minutes, since each is worked out over them. Cancel writes nothing. In interval mode the session fills the time blocks it covered. Discard asks first.

The planned length, alerts, keys and prompt levels are saved with the data file. While a session is open, Load simulation, Open data, Clear all and each behavior’s measurement are locked, and closing the page asks first.

Daily data

Leave a cell blank when the behavior was not measured that day — blank days break the data path rather than plotting as zero. Enter a real 0 when you observed the behavior at zero.

Observation minutes fill in from this window. Type over a day's value when the student arrived late or left early; clear it to go back to the window.

Full phase change starts a new condition: the graph breaks the data path, draws a solid vertical line, and analyzes the new condition separately. Conditional change keeps the condition intact and drops a dashed annotated line at that date — use it for events inside a phase such as a medication adjustment, a staffing change, or an illness. On the graph each name is centered over its section; type | to break a long one onto a new line, as in Summer program | 2025. Dates copied from Google Sheets or Excel can be pasted onto a Date cell (columns to their right fill in too), or entered with Paste dates.

Interval record: no day selected

Enter what happened in each block of the day. The day total is calculated for you and is what the graphs plot. The activity column is one shared schedule — editing it here changes it for every day.

Episode log: no day selected

For a behavior measured by duration, log each episode on its own line: when it started and stopped, or just how long it lasted — type 3:20 for three minutes twenty seconds, 3.5 for minutes, or 45s for seconds. To log one as it happens, press Start one now when it begins and the now beside End when it stops — the length is timed to the second. The day's total on the sheet above is the sum of the episodes, and it is what the graph plots. A day with no episodes logged keeps whatever total you type on the sheet.
