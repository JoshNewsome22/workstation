Your record and what happened

The agreement figures are the ones two observers of the same session would compute: total count (the smaller count over the larger), count by 10-second interval, total duration (the shorter over the longer), and interval by interval. A common convention is 80% or better before a person records alone; your plan may set a higher level. A drawn scene is easier to watch than a classroom: it is practice for the definition and the timing, and it does not replace a check beside a trained observer with the student.
