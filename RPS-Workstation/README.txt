FBA/BIP Workstation - Royal Palm School
=======================================

This is the Royal Palm School edition. It is the same workstation and the same 31
forms as the Newsome Behavioral Health edition; only the logo and name differ, and
it keeps its own autosave, so both can be used in one browser without mixing.

Double-click index.html. Everything runs from this folder.

  - Pick a form from the list on the left (on a narrow window or a tablet, the
    list opens from the Forms button). It opens in the window and STAYS loaded,
    so you can move between forms and come back without losing work.
  - Fill the student bar at the top once. Those details go into each form as
    you open it, filling only fields that are still empty.
  - Tick the forms this student needs. "Build master print" gathers exactly
    those into one document with a cover sheet and contents list, each form
    starting on a new page. Unticked forms are not included.
  - Fullscreen (above the open form, or Ctrl+Shift+F) puts away the heading,
    the student bar and the list so only the form is showing; Esc brings them
    back. On a wide window the list can also be hidden from its own corner and
    brought back with Forms. On a phone the student bar folds behind Packet.

Save case (top bar) writes one file holding every open form and the student
details; Open case brings it all back. Work is also kept on this computer as a
safety net and offered back when you reopen - see SETUP.md. Each form still has
its own Save data button.

A ticked form you have never opened prints blank; the workstation warns you
before that happens.

If anything here misbehaves, each of the 34 forms also works on its own:
double-click its file and it saves, opens and prints exactly as it does inside
the workstation.

To print one form, press Print this form above it (or Ctrl+P / Cmd+P while
working in it). Build master print gathers the ticked forms into one document.

Once the master print is saved as a PDF, Finish PDF (beside it in the top bar)
gives you a copy with a bookmark for every form and each form's page number
printed beside its entry on the contents page. Nothing already on a page is
changed. See SETUP.md.

Press Diagnostics in the top bar for two checks worth running once on your
machine: whether pop-ups are allowed (the master print needs them), and
whether a form's own Print button prints just that form.

The same folder runs from this computer or from a website - there is no
separate website version. To put it on a website, or update the one that is
there, see HOSTING.md.

Form IA-1 can take the answers straight from a Google Form: open the responses
spreadsheet, copy the Form Responses tab, and press Import Google Forms responses
in IA-1's toolbar. Each response becomes an informant column and the worksheet
does the scoring.

One file instead of a folder: NBH-Workstation.html (RPS-Workstation.html for
the school edition) is the whole workstation in a single file, the 34 forms
inside it. Keep it on the district drive and
double-click it. Its Save case writes the case as one file of its own,
Student.case.html, which opens the workstation with that case already in it
when double-clicked; in Chrome and Edge each later save goes back into that
same file. Both editions open either kind of case file.

Form DT-1 (Delay Tolerance) has a Walkthrough tab: the assessments drawn
step by step at the table, practice drills, and a button that prints job aids
with an integrity checklist for each procedure.

Form AD-1 (Accumulated vs. Distributed Reinforcement) compares reinforcement
delivered after every response with reinforcement banked and delivered in one
block: efficacy, the student's own choice, the time each arrangement costs, and
the plan for daily use. It has an evidence guide built from the six studies it
draws on and a Walkthrough tab like DT-1's.

Form MT-1 (Discontinuous Measurement) has a built-in observation timer above
the sheet. It takes its clock and its marks from the observation length and the
ratio, alerts you at each mark (sound, flash, vibration on phones; each can be
turned off), and fills the + / - / N/A you press into the next interval waiting.

Forms PA-1 (Preference Assessment) and RA-1 (Reinforcer Assessment) have a live
runner at the top of each method sheet. PA-1 covers single stimulus, paired
stimulus, MSWO/MSW, free operant, competing stimulus and ecological sheets:
response windows, access timers, rotation, and engagement timing. RA-1 covers
single operant, concurrent operants and progressive ratio: schedules, access
timers, and the stop interval and break point. Each writes its results into the
sheet. Both have a Walkthrough view with the procedures drawn at the table,
practice drills and job aids, and their research statements were checked against
the sources and corrected.

Form EA-1 (Experimental Analysis) has a session runner on its Sessions sheet: the
session clock, the consequence period after each target (for example the 30-s
break in Demand), the control condition's attention schedule, prompt-step cues,
target and precursor counts, latency and trial-based sessions, and Save to the
log. It also has a Walkthrough view with the conditions drawn at the table,
practice drills and job aids, and its research statements were checked against
the sources and corrected.

Form ABC-1 (ABC Recording) has an Observation timer that schedules the background
checks and alerts you at each one, skips a check when an incident was logged in
its window, and fills in the background sample; an incident stopwatch; numbered
tabs; and a Walkthrough tab with drawn scenes, coding drills and job aids. Its
statements about the research were checked against the sources and corrected.

Form MT-1 (Discontinuous Measurement) captures a session into its log from the
data sheet, carrying the session's condition, change marker and notes, and
clears the sheet for the next session without touching the log.

Form SP-1 (Scatter Plot) can hold several target behaviors in one record: press
+ Add a behavior above the sheet and each gets a data sheet, pattern summary and
control chart of its own, on the same days, and they all print together. Its
Marking setting in the toolbar chooses between the slash / shaded (low / high
rate) marks and plain shaded-or-blank marks.
