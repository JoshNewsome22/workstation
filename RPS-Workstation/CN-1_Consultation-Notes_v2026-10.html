What This Form Keeps

A consultation note is the record that a service happened and what it consisted of. It has to serve three readers at once: the BCBA a month later, who needs to know what was recommended and whether anyone did it; the team, who need the changes to the plan and the authority behind them; and anyone who later audits the service, who needs the date, the time, the people, the setting and the signature. The note on this form is arranged in the order of a visit (who and where, what was looked at, what was found, what was advised, what was changed, what was taught, what comes next) so that writing it is a matter of filling the rows, and the log makes every note findable.

Two things on the note are computed rather than written. The duration comes from the start and end times, and the time by category must add up to it, which keeps the categories honest. The week’s rate is compared with the goal in the direction the goal runs, so the note says in one line whether the plan is working on its own terms. The integrity score links the note to the Form TI-1 observation it rests on, since a recommendation to change a plan that is not being run is a different recommendation from one about a plan that is.

What the Research and the Standards Support

Consultation is a problem-solving sequence with data at each stageBergan and Kratochwill (1990) describe behavioral consultation as four stages (problem identification, problem analysis, plan implementation, plan evaluation), each carried by an interview with a defined purpose and each ending in a decision made on data. The note’s order follows that sequence: what the data show, what the problem is, what the plan change is, what is evaluated next. Erchul and Martens (2010) review the evidence for the model in schools and the conditions under which consultee behavior changes; the training and next-steps rows are where those conditions are recorded.
The family belongs in the sequenceConjoint behavioral consultation (Sheridan, Kratochwill & Bergan, 1996) runs the same four stages with the parent and the teacher together. A parent meeting on this form is a note of the same shape as a school consultation, so a home decision and a school decision sit in one log.
Recommendations without follow-up are not implementedNoell et al. (2005) followed the plans written for 45 elementary students referred for consultation, each with their teacher, and compared weekly follow-up meetings, a commitment emphasis, and performance feedback on the teacher’s own implementation data. Implementation held up only under performance feedback, and student outcomes followed. On this form the integrity score, the next steps with owner and date, and the open-steps table on the Log are the performance-feedback loop; a note that records a recommendation without a date and an owner has, on this evidence, recorded a wish.
The Ethics Code requires the recordThe Behavior Analyst Certification Board’s (2020) Ethics Code for Behavior Analysts requires behavior analysts to create and maintain documentation of their professional work, to protect and retain it, to report their services accurately, and to collect data and evaluate interventions continually (sections 2.05, 2.06, 2.17 and 2.18 in the Code’s numbering). The date, times, setting, people, signature and time-by-category rows exist so that each note satisfies those provisions on its face, and the log keeps the retention record.
Decisions are made on the data, in the direction of the goalCooper, Heron and Heward (2020) set out data-based decision making: a measure compared with a criterion, a trend read before a change, and the change recorded with its date so the graph can show what it did. The rate-against-goal line and the plan-in-force row are the note’s share of that record; the graphs themselves live on the data forms the note cites.

How the Figures Are Calculated

DurationEnd time minus start time, in minutes. Times are read as h:mm with an optional am or pm; a time without am or pm is read on the 24-hour clock, and an end time earlier than the start is taken as the afternoon of the same day.
Time by categoryThe seven category fields are summed and compared with the duration. A sum within 5 minutes of the duration is accepted; otherwise the note says by how much the two differ (a working convention; travel and documentation are often outside the start-to-end span, and the note says so when they are).
Rate against goalFor a behavior to reduce, the goal is met when the rate is at or below it; for a skill to build, when the rate is at or above it. “Close” means within 25% of the goal on the wrong side (a working convention).
IntegrityThe TI-1 score as a percent: 90% and above is treated as acceptable, 80% to 89% as needing a retraining step, below 80% as a plan that is not being run (working conventions, the same as Form TI-1 uses).
Hours by monthEach note’s duration, in hours to two decimals, summed by the month of its date and by its type. Notes without a readable date are listed under “undated”.
Open next stepsSteps not ticked done, from every note; a step is overdue when its date is before today.
Service log entries (v21.85)Each dated note is one entry. Its minutes are the service minutes typed on the note, else the duration from the start and end times; its kind is the one chosen, else the type’s (an observation is observation, a consultation or a call is consultation, a training is training, a direct session is direct service, a parent or IEP meeting is a meeting, a record review is other), else the category with the most minutes; with whom is the one typed, else the roles named in brackets under who was present (a parent meeting is with the parent, an IEP meeting with the team, a direct session with the student); the one line is the one typed, else the purpose. A note without a date, or without minutes or times, is not an entry.
This week, this month, the IEPThe week runs Monday to Sunday and the month is the calendar month, both the ones containing today. Against the IEP’s minutes (Form DM-1, per week, month or grading period, between the dates typed for the grading period), the minutes counted are those of the kind the IEP names: direct service for direct, every other kind (consultation, observation, training, meetings, other) for consultation, and all of them for both. “120 of 120 minutes this month” is the minutes counted against the minutes owed.

What Makes a Note Hold Up

Written the same dayA note written from memory a week later is weaker as a record and less useful as a plan. The “note written on” row is there so a late note says it is late.
Observation separated from judgment“What was seen” is what happened, in order; “Problems identified” is what it means. A reader should be able to disagree with the second while accepting the first.
Changes name their authorityA plan change inside the adjustments the plan already allows is the BCBA’s call; one outside them is the team’s (Form PR-1) or needs consent (Form IC-1). The note says which, every time.
Every recommendation has a next stepA recommendation that nobody owns by a date is recorded as “recommended only; awaiting the team” and reappears on the open-steps table until it is either adopted or dropped.
The note cites its dataWhich forms, which dates. The note does not repeat the graph; it says where the graph is.
The service log stands on the notesThe printed service log is a summary of the notes, not a substitute for them: each line points to a dated, signed note that says what the service consisted of. Where the district bills school-based Medicaid, it sets which fields its log needs (commonly the student and Medicaid ID, the date, the start and end times or the minutes, the service, the provider’s name and credential, and a signature); check the printed page against the district’s own requirements before using it as the billing record.
Nothing about third parties that is not neededOther students are not named; a peer is “a peer”. Staff are named with their role because the plan assigns them steps.

Writing help

A multi-line text box on this form shows an Improve wording button at its corner when you are in it and whenever it holds text; from a keyboard, Alt+Enter in the box (Option+Return on a Mac or iPad) opens the same panel. Check wording works on this device and sends nothing: it marks words that name a feeling, guess at intent, function or a diagnosis, label the behavior or the person, or leave a count, time or intensity vague, and suggests observable wording; words in quotation marks are left alone. On an iPad with Apple Intelligence, Writing Tools (select the text) can also proofread and rewrite; it is Apple’s own and works on the text as written, names included, on the iPad or on Apple’s servers, so follow your agency’s rules for it and do not use its ChatGPT options for student text. The button and the panel are not saved with the form and never print.

About the simulation

Load simulation (toolbar) replaces every entry with a worked example; nothing in it is real data. The simulated case is six notes over five weeks (two observations with TI-1 scores, a phone call, a parent meeting, an IEP meeting and a consultation), with next steps, time by category and the hours log; the service log counts them against an IEP of 120 minutes a month of consultation.
